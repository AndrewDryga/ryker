Publish and discard run the identical service handlers the Slack buttons call — the Coop review, the verified discard plan, the audit record. Their outcome notice is posted to the room's Slack channel exactly as a button press would be, and the row here changes once cleanup records the result.

No workspace is waiting on you. One lands here when automatic cleanup refuses a fork for a stated reason — a dirty tree, unpublished commits, a Coop conflict — and it stays until an operator publishes it, discards it, or sends it back through the checks.
